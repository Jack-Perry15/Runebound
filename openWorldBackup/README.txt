Open world backup (removed 2026-10-05)

(Game.java.txt becomes Game.java and replaces the new Game.java), and keep the grid pngs and level
txt files that are still in the Runebound folder (swampGrid.png, emberGrid.png, cavernsGrid.png,
canopyGrid.png and the four *Level.txt files).
